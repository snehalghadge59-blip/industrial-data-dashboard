Ministry of Ayush (ayush.gov.in)

Govt. of India Official Portal

Official national health portal of the Ministry of Ayush, Government of India, regulating traditional healthcare systems: Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa, and Homoeopathy. The portal provides pharmacopeial medicine standards, National Medicinal Plants Board (NMPB) guidelines, AYUSH clinical trial registries, and certified drug manufacturing licenses. MediFinder integrates with AYUSH standards to verify authentic local Ayurvedic and Homeopathy pharmacies across PCMC.
